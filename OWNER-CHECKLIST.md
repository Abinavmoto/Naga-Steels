## 5. Two things that will bring you more business than the website

Being honest with you about where the results actually come from:

### Your Google listing matters more than this website
When someone searches "chain link fencing Madurai", Google shows a **map box with three
businesses at the very top**, above all the normal results. Getting into that box is decided
almost entirely by your Google Business Profile, not by your website.

You already have a 5.0 rating, which is excellent. Two things would help most:
- [ ] **Add photos** of your godown, the stock, the shopfront and finished fencing jobs. Google
      strongly favours listings with real, recent photos.
- [ ] **Ask customers for reviews.** After a delivery goes well, send them the review link on
      WhatsApp. Ten reviews will do more for your phone ringing than anything on this site.

### Real photos of your yard
The applications section now uses six photographs taken from your previous website. Each one
there carried the note *"Conceptual application visual only. It does not represent a completed
Naga Steels project."* I have carried that disclaimer onto the new site, so nothing claims to
be your work when it is not.

They are good, relevant pictures, but they are not **yours**. In this trade a photo of actual
stock in an actual godown converts better than any design work, and it is the one thing a
competitor cannot copy.

If you can send me:
- [ ] The shopfront / signboard
