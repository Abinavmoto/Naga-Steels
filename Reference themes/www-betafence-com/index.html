Complete perimeter solutions

Perimeter Protection Solutions for Residential, Commercial & Critical Infrastructure

Betafence develops perimeter protection solutions for residential, commercial, industrial and critical infrastructure projects worldwide. The portfolio covers the complete site boundary with perimeter fencing, welded mesh fencing, fence panels, posts, privacy systems, pedestrian and vehicle gates, access control systems, high-security fencing, crash-rated gates, anti-ram protection, bollards, road blockers, hostile vehicle mitigation and passive anti-drone systems. Solutions can be selected according to appearance, operational use, required security level, regional availability and applicable certification requirements.

Residential solutions

Residential systems are developed around secure outdoor living, privacy, design and straightforward installation. Matching components allow the fence line, access points and privacy elements to form one coherent system around the property.

  • Architectural and professional post systems
  • Rigid panel fencing systems
  • Privacy slats, boards and infills
  • Residential swing and sliding gates
  • Decorative fencing and gabion concepts
  • Welded mesh and fencing rolls

Commercial solutions

Commercial perimeter systems are designed for reliable daily performance around schools, warehouses, logistics sites, business parks, public buildings and industrial facilities. The range combines durable fence lines with compatible posts, gates and privacy options.

  • Professional 3D and twin-wire panel systems
  • Anti-climb commercial mesh fencing
  • Post systems for different fixing methods and terrain
  • Commercial swing and sliding gates
  • Privacy solutions for compatible panel systems
  • Decorative systems and fencing rolls

Perimeter protection

Perimeter protection solutions address forced entry, climbing, cutting, vehicle intrusion and drone threats. They are developed for sites where protection must be matched to operational risks, access requirements and recognised security standards.

  • Certified high-security fencing systems
  • High-security swing, sliding and speed gates
  • Crash-rated fencing and integrated HVM systems
  • Static, retractable and shallow-foundation bollards
  • Crash-rated road wedges and road blockers
  • Passive low-level and lateral anti-drone protection

One perimeter, multiple layers of protection

A complete perimeter is more than a fence. The required solution can combine boundary definition, anti-climb and anti-cut performance, controlled pedestrian or vehicle access, hostile vehicle mitigation and protection against unmanned aerial threats. Betafence systems are therefore organised around the way a site is used and the threats it must resist.

Intrusion resistance

High-security welded mesh, anti-climb panels, reinforced fencing and certified delay systems are used where resistance to climbing, cutting, grinding or forced entry is required.

Controlled access

Swing gates, cantilever and tracked sliding gates, speed folding gates and other controlled access systems secure entrances while supporting the operational flow of people and vehicles.

Hostile vehicle mitigation

Crash-rated fencing, gates, barriers, bollards and road wedges are engineered to reduce the risk of hostile or unauthorised vehicle intrusion at sensitive sites and public-facing environments.

Anti-drone protection

Passive interception systems provide a physical layer of protection against low-level and lateral drone threats before they can reach critical assets or operational zones.

Certified performance for demanding projects

Depending on the selected system and market, the Betafence portfolio includes solutions tested or certified against recognised standards for forced-entry resistance, anti-personnel protection and hostile vehicle mitigation. Certification and regional availability must always be matched to the specific project requirement.

  • ASTM F2781
  • LPS 1175
  • PAS 68
  • ISO 22343
  • ASTM F2656
  • HCIS

Frequently asked questions

What types of fencing and perimeter protection does Betafence offer?

Betafence organises its offer in three main groups: residential fencing for homes and gardens; commercial fencing for schools, public buildings, business parks, warehouses and industrial sites; and perimeter protection for critical or sensitive locations requiring higher resistance to intrusion, vehicle attack or drone threats.

Which Betafence solutions are suitable for homes and gardens?

Residential solutions include welded panel and roll fencing, compatible posts, privacy infills, decorative systems and matching pedestrian or vehicle gates. The choice depends mainly on the required privacy, appearance, boundary function and installation method.

Which Betafence solutions are suitable for commercial and industrial sites?

For commercial and industrial sites, Betafence offers welded-mesh panel systems, professional posts, anti-climb options, privacy solutions and matching swing or sliding gates. A standard commercial system is suitable where the main needs are boundary definition, durability and controlled access; higher-risk sites should be assessed for high-security protection.

What is the difference between commercial fencing and high-security perimeter protection?

Commercial fencing provides a durable boundary and controlled access for normal site operations. High-security perimeter protection is selected where the risk assessment requires resistance to climbing, cutting, grinding or forced entry, or protection against hostile vehicles and drones.

Which Betafence solutions are used for critical and sensitive sites?

Critical and sensitive sites can combine certified high-security fencing, high-security or crash-rated gates, hostile vehicle mitigation such as crash-rated fences, barriers, bollards and road blockers, and passive anti-drone systems. The final design must match the identified threats, access requirements and operational conditions of the site.

Can Betafence fencing, gates and access control be combined into one system?

Yes. Compatible panels, posts, pedestrian gates, vehicle gates and access-control elements can be specified as one perimeter system. For higher-risk projects, the fence line can also be integrated with certified intrusion-delay, hostile vehicle mitigation or passive anti-drone solutions.

Are all Betafence products available and certified in every market?

No. Product availability, dimensions, configurations, certifications and guarantees vary by region and by system. Standards such as LPS 1175, ASTM F2781, ASTM F2656, PAS 68, ISO 22343 or HCIS should only be stated when they apply to the exact product and configuration selected for the project.

From residential boundaries and commercial sites to critical infrastructure, Betafence brings compatible fencing, gates and protection technologies together around the required use, risk level and regional availability.
