The business

A supplier, not a catalogue.

Naga Steels is a steel distributor, wholesaler and metal supplier based at 124 Chitrakara Street in Madurai. The business has traded since 2002 and registered for GST in 2022.

What that means in practice is that the stock is held rather than ordered in. Collection from the godown is usually same-day, and the six product families sit under one roof so a single enquiry can close an entire material schedule instead of three.

Naga Fencing is the dedicated brand for the fencing side. Chain link is woven to the wire size, mesh opening and height specified by the customer, which is the clearest difference between this business and a merchant reselling whatever the mill produced.

The rest is supply: GI and MS sheet and plate, GI and steel wire and wire rope, woven and welded mesh, colour-coated and GI roofing sheet, and the nails, bolts and fittings that otherwise hold up a delivery.

How we quote

Honest about scope.

We are a material supplier and a fencing manufacturer. We supply chain link, wire, mesh, sheet, roofing and fixings, and we review drawings and BOQs against them. We do not treat material supply, civil work and installation as the same thing, and we will say so rather than quote for work we do not perform.

What we hold

Six product families.

  1. 01

    Fencing systems. Chain link in GI, PVC-coated and heavy-duty, plus barbed wire.

  2. 02

    Sheets and plate. GI plain and shutter sheet, MS sheet and steel plate, cut to size.

  3. 03

    Wire and rope. GI wire 8 to 18 SWG, binding wire and steel wire rope.

  4. 04

    Mesh. Woven wire mesh and welded mesh in rolls and rigid panels.

  5. 05

    Roofing. Colour-coated, galvalume and GI sheet cut to rafter length.

  6. 06

    Hardware. Nails, concrete nails, GI bolts and fencing fittings.

Brands & partners

Who we supply.

Naga Steels is the master brand across all six families.

Naga Fencing is the fencing brand, covering chain link variants, barbed wire and boundary material.

Tata Wiron galvanized, binding and fencing wire is supplied as a dealer.

VNC Group products are supplied as a distributor, including Bestfence, Bestwire, Bestarc and Voltwire. Availability and current manufacturer specifications are confirmed at the time of quotation.
