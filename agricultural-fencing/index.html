Application · 01

Fencing for agricultural land.

Boundary material for farms, plots and agricultural land around Madurai, where cost per running foot over a long perimeter is what decides the specification.

Scope Material supply

We supply the fencing material. Civil work, foundations and erection are a separate scope.

The requirement

A farm boundary is an arithmetic problem before it is a material problem. The perimeter is long, the budget is per acre rather than per foot, and the fence has to survive weather and livestock with no maintenance for years.

That usually points to one of two answers: barbed wire where the job is demarcation, or chain link where the job is exclusion. The mistake is specifying for the wrong one and paying twice.

Recommended specification

  1. 01

    Long perimeter, demarcation. Barbed wire at five to seven strands. Lowest cost per running foot by a wide margin.

  2. 02

    Livestock exclusion. 10 SWG chain link at 2" mesh. Handles cattle pressure without bellying.

  3. 03

    Crop protection. 12 SWG chain link at 1" to 1½" against smaller animals.

  4. 04

    Coastal districts. PVC coated, or move up a zinc grade. Salt air is unforgiving.

  5. 05

    Gates and access. Plan the gate positions before ordering; corner and gate struts change the post count.

Specifying it

What to get right first time.

Zinc is where a cheap agricultural quote usually economises, because the difference is invisible on the day of delivery. A commercial coating at 40 to 60 GSM and a heavy coating at 200 to 275 GSM look identical when the fence goes up, and are completely different materials four monsoons later.

For a field boundary that will not be inspected or maintained, specify the heavy coating. The premium is small against the cost of re-fencing, and nobody wants to re-run a kilometre of boundary.

Freight matters disproportionately on agricultural work because the quantities are large and the sites are rural. We quote the material and the transport separately so you can decide whether collection from Madurai makes more sense than delivery.

The material estimator on the homepage will work out the rolls, line posts and corner struts from your boundary length before you send the enquiry.
