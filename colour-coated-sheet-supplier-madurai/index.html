Roofing · Supplied

Colour-coated sheets supplied in Madurai.

Corrugated colour-coated sheet, galvalume and GI roofing sheet with ridge caps and flashing. Cut to your rafter length so there is no overlap waste on site.

Supply scope Cut to length

Send the rafter length and the number of sheets. Colour availability is confirmed at the time of quotation.

Overview

Roofing sheet is one of the few materials where buying it cut to length genuinely changes the cost of the job. Standard lengths mean overlaps you did not need, and every unnecessary overlap is sheet you paid for and then hid.

Give us the rafter length and the sheet count and the roof arrives in the pieces it needs to be, with the ridge and flashing to close it.

Buying guide

Thickness, span and what actually fails.

Roofing sheet almost never fails by rusting through in the field. It fails at the fixings, at the laps and at the cut edges, and it fails early if the sheet was too light for the purlin spacing and has been flexing in the wind for two years.

So the thickness should be chosen from the span, not from the budget. For an agricultural godown or a domestic shed, 0.45 to 0.50 mm is the usual answer. For a factory shed with a longer purlin span, 0.55 mm or above.

Near the coast, specify a better coating rather than a thicker sheet. Salt attacks the coating long before it troubles the steel.

Questions before an enquiry

Useful answers.

What thickness roofing sheet should I use for a shed?

For an agricultural godown or a domestic shed, 0.45 to 0.50 mm colour-coated sheet is the usual specification. For a factory shed or anywhere with a longer purlin span, go to 0.55 mm or above.

Do you cut sheets to my rafter length?

Yes. Cutting to length means you do not pay for overlap waste. Send the rafter length and the number of sheets.

Do you stock galvalume as well as colour coated?

Galvalume and GI roofing sheet are both part of the range. Availability of a specific grade and colour is confirmed during quotation.

Do you supply ridge caps and screws?

Ridge caps, flashing and fixings are supplied alongside the sheet so the roof arrives complete on one invoice.
