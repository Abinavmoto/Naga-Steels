Before you send

      
Four things that get you a rate first time.

      
    
        
  1. 01
    The product. Chain link, weld mesh, GI sheet, roofing, binding wire, and so on.
    2. 
        
  2. 02
    The specification. Gauge and mesh for fencing; thickness and size for sheet; profile and length for roofing.
    3. 
        
  3. 03
    The quantity. Running feet, square feet, kilograms or rolls. An approximation is fine.
    4. 
        
  4. 04
    The delivery location. So freight can be quoted separately rather than buried in the rate.
    5. 
      

    

    

      
Also useful

      
Drawings, BOQs and photographs.

      

        A drawing, a bill of quantities or a reference photograph of the boundary converts a
        general request into a line-item schedule you can compare against another quote.
        For project work this is usually faster than describing it in a message.

        grade that the location does not justify.
