Overview

Made to spec, not sold from stock.

Most fencing in this market is bought in and resold. The merchant holds whatever the mill produced, and the customer takes the nearest thing to what they asked for.

Manufacturing to specification changes that conversation. You give us the wire size, the mesh opening, the finished height, the roll length and the quantity, and that is what gets woven. Where the requested specification is not practical, we say so before the order rather than after.

Naga Steels remains the master brand across all six product families. Naga Fencing is the name for the fencing work specifically, because that is the enquiry people arrive with.

What we need from you

Five things to weave to spec.

  1. 01

    Wire gauge. In SWG. 8, 10 or 12 for chain link.

  2. 02

    Mesh opening. 1", 1½", 2", 2½" or 3".

  3. 03

    Finished height. 3 ft to 8 ft.

  4. 04

    Running length. Total feet, and the roll length you want it cut to.

  5. 05

    Coating. Hot-dip GI, PVC coated or electro-galvanized, and the zinc grade if it is specified.

Scope

Where our work ends.

We manufacture and supply the fencing material. We supply chain link, barbed wire, tension wire, bolts and fittings, and we will review a drawing or a BOQ against them.

We do not quote for post setting, concrete, excavation or erection. Material supply, civil work and installation are three different trades, and treating them as one is how a quotation becomes a dispute.

If you need the fence erected as well as supplied, say so and we will tell you plainly that it is outside what we do, rather than subcontracting a trade we cannot stand behind.
