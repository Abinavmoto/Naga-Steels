Wire & rope · Supplied

GI wire supplied from Madurai.

Galvanized iron wire from 8 to 18 SWG in commercial and heavy zinc coating, rebar binding wire, fence tension and line wire, and steel wire rope for rigging and lifting. Supplied by the coil or by weight.

Supply scope Stocked & sourced

Common gauges are held in stock. Heavier coating grades and specific coil weights are confirmed against availability at the time of quotation.

Overview

Wire is the least glamorous line on a material schedule and the one most likely to hold up a pour. Binding wire runs out, the site stops, and somebody drives to Madurai.

We hold the common gauges rather than ordering them in, which is the entire reason collection is usually same-day. For anything unusual, tell us the gauge and the coating grade and we will confirm availability before you commit to a date.

  3. 03

  4. 04

  5. 05

    General fabrication. Tying, bracing and temporary restraint on site.

Buying guide

Gauge, coating, and why the cheap coil is cheap.

Wire is bought by weight but used by length. A lighter gauge gives you more metres per kilogram, which makes the invoice look better and the job go further, right up until the wire is too light for what it is holding.

The second variable is zinc. Commercial coating at 40 to 60 GSM is fine indoors or for short-term site work. Anything outdoors and permanent wants 200 GSM or above, and near the coast it wants more than that.

If a coil is noticeably cheaper than the market, it is almost always one of those two things rather than a better deal.

Questions before an enquiry

What gauges of GI wire do you stock?

The usual range is 8 to 18 SWG. Common gauges are held in stock; unusual gauges and coating grades are confirmed against availability at the time of quotation.

Binding wire is supplied in the gauges and finishes commonly used for rebar tying. Tell us which you need and the quantity, and we will confirm what is available.
