Specification · Standards

      
What actually decides how long a fence lasts.

      

        Almost every complaint about fencing comes down to one of three things: too little zinc,
        too light a gauge, or the wrong coating for the location. Here is how to read a quote
        so you can tell whether you are being offered the same fence twice.
      

    


    

      

        IS 4826
        
Zinc coating, in GSM

        
The single number that decides lifespan. A 40 GSM commercial coating and a 275 GSM
           heavy coating look identical on the day they are installed. Four years later they
           do not. Always ask for the GSM in writing.

      

      

        IS 280
        
Gauge, not "thickness"

        
Wire is sold by SWG. A drop from 10 SWG to 12 SWG removes about a third of the steel
           from every foot of fence. If one quote is unusually cheap, check the gauge before
           you check the rate.

      

      

        IS 278
        
Barb spacing

        
Barbed wire is priced by weight, so a wider barb spacing means fewer barbs and less
           wire per kilogram. Compare the barb interval, not just the coil weight.

Procurement · FAQ

      
The questions we get on WhatsApp every week.

    


    

      

        What is the minimum order for chain link fencing in Madurai?
        
There is no fixed minimum for collection from our Chitrakkara Street godown, because we cut
        from the roll. For delivery to other districts a lorry load is typically 500 kg or more,
        because below that the freight per kilogram stops making sense for you. Tell us your running
        feet on WhatsApp and we will confirm whether it is worth delivering or better collected.

      

      

        Which gauge chain link should I use for farm fencing?
        
10 SWG (3.25 mm) in a 2 inch mesh is the standard choice for
        agricultural boundary fencing and handles cattle pressure well. For a light garden or
        poultry boundary, 12 SWG (2.64 mm) is enough and costs less. For industrial perimeter,
        solar parks or anywhere security matters, use 8 SWG (4.06 mm).

      

      

        Hot-dip galvanized or PVC coated: which should I buy?
        
Hot-dip galvanized wire is dipped in molten zinc so the coating bonds to the steel
        and covers every cut end and knuckle, giving roughly 15–25 years outdoors. PVC coated wire
        has a galvanized core sealed inside a plastic jacket, lasting 20–30 years and performing
        better in coastal or high-humidity conditions such as Ramanathapuram and Tirunelveli. PVC
        costs more per foot but is usually the cheaper choice over the life of the fence near the
        sea.

      

      

        Do you deliver outside Madurai?
        
Yes. We regularly dispatch to Dindigul, Virudhunagar, Theni, Sivakasi, Karaikudi,
        Aruppukottai, Usilampatti, Ramanathapuram and Tirunelveli. Nearby towns such as Melur and
        Thirumangalam can often be same-day. Send your delivery pin on WhatsApp and we will quote
        the material and the freight separately.

      

      

        Is chain link sold by weight or by length?
        
Chain link is normally priced by weight but quoted to you in running
        feet at a given height, because that is how you buy it. The weight for a given length depends
        on the gauge and the aperture. A heavier gauge or a tighter mesh puts more steel into the
        same running foot. Always compare quotes at the same gauge and the same aperture, otherwise
        you are not comparing the same fence.

      

      

        How many posts do I need for my boundary?
        
Posts are normally set at 10 ft centres for standard chain link, so the count is your
        perimeter divided by 10, plus one to close the run, plus extra struts at each corner and
        gate. The material calculator above works this out for you along
        with the number of rolls.

      

      

        What thickness roofing sheet should I use for a shed?
        
For an agricultural godown or a domestic shed, 0.45–0.50 mm
        colour-coated sheet is the usual specification. For a factory shed or a longer purlin span,
        go to 0.55 mm or above. We cut sheets to your rafter length so you do not pay for overlap
        waste. See roofing sheets for the full range.
