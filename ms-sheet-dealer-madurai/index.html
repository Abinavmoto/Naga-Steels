Overview

Mild steel sheet and plate is the base material for most fabrication in and around Madurai: gates, frames, brackets, base plates, covers and anything that will be welded and painted.

The useful question is rarely "what does a sheet cost" but "what does my cutting list cost". Send the list and we will price it against stock, which usually comes out lower than buying full sheets and carrying the offcut.

Buying guide

Specifying plate without over-buying.

Plate is heavy, and weight is price. Going one thickness up "to be safe" on a large piece can add a meaningful amount to the invoice for strength the job will never use.

If the piece is structural, the thickness should come from the drawing or the engineer, not from the merchant. If it is not structural, the thinnest sheet that will not oil-can or dent in service is usually the right answer.

MS will rust. It is supplied in mill finish and needs paint or galvanizing if it is going outside. If it is going outside and will not be painted, buy GI sheet instead.

Questions before an enquiry

Useful answers.

What thickness of MS sheet and plate do you supply?

Broadly 1 mm to 6 mm. Confirm the exact thickness and piece sizes with your enquiry so availability can be checked.

Can you quote against a cutting list?

Yes. Send the list or a drawing and we will quote it line by line rather than by the full sheet, which usually comes out lower than buying full sheets and carrying the offcut.

Is MS sheet supplied painted?

No, it is supplied in mill finish. If the piece is going outdoors unpainted, galvanized sheet is the better choice.

What is the difference between sheet and plate?

It is a thickness convention rather than a different material. Thinner material is referred to as sheet, heavier material as plate, and both are quoted by weight.
