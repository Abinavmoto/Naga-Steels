Privacy.

Short, because there is very little to say.

What this website collects

Nothing. This is a static website. There is no server, no database, no contact form that posts anywhere, no analytics script and no tracking cookie.

The quote builder

The quote builder on this site runs entirely in your browser. It assembles the text of a WhatsApp message from the options you select and places that text into a link. Nothing is transmitted anywhere until you choose to tap send, at which point the message goes to WhatsApp in the normal way and WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply.

The material estimator and the fence configurator work the same way: everything is computed locally and nothing leaves your device.

Local storage

The site does not store personal information on your device. No advertising or analytics identifiers are set.

When you contact us

If you call, send a WhatsApp message or email nagasteelsindia@gmail.com, we keep what you send for as long as it takes to quote and fulfil the enquiry, and for the record-keeping any business is required to maintain. We do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a list.

Third parties

Links to Google Maps and WhatsApp leave this site. Once you follow them, those services apply their own terms. Fonts are self-hosted on this domain specifically so that loading a page here does not send a request to a third party.
