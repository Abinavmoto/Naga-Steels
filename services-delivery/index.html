Services & delivery

From specification to scheduled supply.

Clear scope, usable buying details and delivery planning — without treating material supply, civil work and installation as the same thing.

Scope boundary Supply & manufacture

We manufacture chain link and supply steel, mesh and hardware. We do not quote for civil work, foundations or erection.

What we do

Four services, plainly stated.

Each one is a thing we actually perform. Anything outside this list we will tell you we do not do, rather than subcontract a trade we cannot stand behind.

01

Made-to-spec chain-link

Chain link is woven to the specification you give us rather than sold from whatever happens to be on the shelf. Tell us the gauge, the mesh opening, the finished height and the running length, and we confirm what we can weave and in what lead time.

02

Material supply

Six product families on one invoice and, where quantities allow, one vehicle. Holding stock rather than ordering it in is what makes same-day collection from Madurai possible.

03

Requirement review

Send a drawing, a bill of quantities or even a photograph of the boundary. We will come back with a line-item schedule you can actually compare against another quote, at the same gauge and the same coating.

04

Dispatch coordination

Freight is quoted separately from material so you can always see what the transport is costing. Vehicle access and unloading are checked before a dispatch date is committed, because a lorry that cannot turn into the site is a wasted day for both of us.

Delivery coverage

Madurai first. Project routes reviewed.

Availability, transport charges, unloading arrangements and delivery dates are confirmed for each order. Freight is always quoted separately from material, so you can see what the transport is costing and decide whether collection makes more sense.

How an order runs

Enquiry to dispatch.

  1. 01

    Send the requirement. Product, dimensions or wire specification, approximate quantity and delivery location.

  2. 02

    Specification confirmed. We check gauge, coating, size and availability, and flag anything that will cost you more than it needs to.

  3. 03

    Rate and freight quoted. Material and transport are priced separately, so the comparison is honest.

  4. 04

    Dispatch or collection. Access, unloading and the required date are agreed before the vehicle is loaded.

What we do not do

The scope boundary.

We are a material supplier and a fencing manufacturer. That is the whole of it.

We do not quote for post setting, concrete, excavation or erection. Material supply, civil work and installation are three different trades, and treating them as one is how a quotation becomes a dispute halfway through a job.

If you need the fence erected as well as supplied, say so and we will tell you plainly that it is outside what we do, rather than subcontracting a trade we cannot stand behind.
