Application · 04

Perimeter material for solar sites.

Project-based boundary material for solar and infrastructure locations, usually driven by an approved drawing or a bill of quantities rather than a rate per foot.

Scope Material supply

We supply the fencing material against your schedule. Civil work, foundations and erection are a separate scope.

The requirement

Solar perimeters are long, remote and specified by somebody who is not on site. The fence is usually in the BOQ before the land is cleared, and the material has to match that schedule exactly or the certification becomes somebody's problem.

So the useful thing we can do is quote against the schedule as written, line by line, rather than offer a rate per running foot that then has to be reconciled.

Recommended specification

  1. 01

    Standard perimeter. 8 to 10 SWG chain link at 2" mesh, heavy zinc.

  2. 02

    Coating grade. 200 to 275 GSM. These fences are never maintained.

  3. 03

    Topping. Three-strand barbed wire where the schedule calls for it.

  4. 04

    Phased delivery. Material released against the construction programme.

  5. 05

    Documentation. Specification confirmed in writing against the BOQ line.

Specifying it

What to get right first time.

Zinc grade is the line item worth protecting on a solar job. The fence is installed once and then left for twenty-five years with no inspection regime, so anything below a heavy coating is a false economy that someone else will inherit.

Phased release matters as much as the specification. A solar site does not want the full perimeter material on day one, so tell us the programme and we will dispatch against it rather than against the order date.

Freight is quoted separately, which on a remote site is worth seeing explicitly rather than buried in the material rate.
