Nails, bolts and fencing hardware.

Mild steel wire nails and concrete nails by size and weight, GI nuts, bolts and fencing fittings, and the small items that otherwise hold up a delivery.

Overview

Nobody plans a project around fasteners, and almost every project is delayed by them at least once. The sheet arrives, the mesh arrives, and the bolts are on a different lorry.

Carrying nails, bolts and fencing fittings alongside the material is not a separate business for us, it is the thing that stops a delivery being incomplete.

Buying guide

Galvanized or plain: a small decision with a long tail.

A plain steel bolt in an outdoor fence will seize or rust within a couple of monsoons, and the cost of replacing it is never the cost of the bolt. It is the trip, the labour and the fence being open in the meantime.

For anything outdoors, specify galvanized. The premium on the fastener is trivial against the material it is holding together.

Nails are bought by weight and used by count, so when you estimate, work out the count you need and then ask us what that weighs rather than guessing at a kilogram figure.

Questions before an enquiry

Useful answers.

What nail sizes do you stock?

MS wire nails in the common sizes, supplied by weight. Confirm the sizes and quantities with your enquiry.

Do you supply galvanized bolts?

GI nuts and bolts are part of the range. For anything outdoors, galvanized is worth specifying because the premium on the fastener is trivial against the material it is holding together.

Can hardware be added to a material order?

Yes, and that is the point of carrying it. Hardware goes on the same invoice and the same vehicle as the material.

How is hardware priced?

By weight for nails and by item or weight for fasteners, depending on the product.
