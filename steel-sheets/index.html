GI sheet or MS sheet?

They are the same steel underneath. The difference is the zinc.

GI (galvanized iron) sheet has been hot-dip coated in zinc, so it resists rust without paint. Use it anywhere the sheet will see weather or damp: roofing, ducting, water tanks, shutters, outdoor cladding. It is more expensive per kilogram and it cannot be welded as cleanly, because the heat burns the zinc off around the weld and that bare strip becomes the first place rust starts.

MS (mild steel) sheet is uncoated. It is cheaper, it welds and paints beautifully, and it will rust within weeks if you leave it out in the open. Use it for fabrication that will be painted or powder-coated afterwards, for structural work, and for anything indoors.

The usual mistake runs in one direction: buying MS to save money on something that lives outdoors. The paint fails at the edges within two monsoons and the piece is scrap. If it is going outside and it will not be repainted on a schedule, buy GI.

Above roughly 3 mm the trade stops calling it sheet and starts calling it plate. That is not a formal boundary, but it matters when you enquire, because plate is priced, stocked and cut differently.

Rolling shutter sheet

A distinct product, not just GI sheet in a different thickness. It arrives pre-formed into interlocking slats and is specified by the shutter opening rather than by sheet dimensions.

Give us the clear width and height of the opening and whether it is manual or motorised. We will work out the slat count and the sheet requirement. Guessing at this from sheet sizes is how shutters end up two slats short.

Cut to size, and why it saves money

Sheet is sold by weight, and a standard sheet is a standard sheet whether or not your job uses all of it. If you need 3 ft × 4 ft pieces, buying full sheets and cutting on site means paying for the offcut, handling it, and finding somewhere to put it.

We cut from stock. Send the finished dimensions and the quantity, and that is what you pay for. It also means the pieces arrive with a clean sheared edge instead of a gas-cut one, which matters if they are going to be welded or painted.

What to send when you enquire

  • Type: GI plain, GI rolling shutter, MS, or plate
  • Thickness in millimetres, or the gauge if that is how you know it
  • Finished size you want the pieces cut to, and how many
  • Delivery town, or tell us you are collecting

If you have a drawing or a cutting list, send a photograph of it. It is faster than typing dimensions and it removes the chance of a transcription error on our side.
