Product detail

What each brand covers.

Specify the brand, the product and the quantity. For electrodes also give the type and the size, since that is a consumable bought by the packet.

Bestfence

Chain-link and fencing systems. Named on schedules where a branded fencing system is specified rather than a generic mesh. If your requirement is generic rather than brand-specified, our own Naga Fencing chain link is woven to your exact gauge and mesh opening.

Bestwire

GI, binding and general wire products. The usual enquiry is a gauge, a coating grade and a quantity by weight. See also our GI wire range.

Bestarc

Welding electrodes. Specify the electrode type and the size with the enquiry. This is a consumable bought by the packet, so the quantity matters as much as the specification.

Voltwire

Electric-fence wire products, for agricultural and perimeter applications where an energised line is part of the design. Tell us the run length and whether the energiser is already specified.
