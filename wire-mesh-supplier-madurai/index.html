Overview

Woven wire mesh is flexible, which is both its advantage and its limit. It will follow a curved surface and it rolls up for transport, but it will not stand on its own as a rigid panel.

That makes it the right choice for plastering, screening, poultry enclosures and anywhere the mesh is supported by something else, and the wrong choice for a free-standing partition. For that, weld mesh is the answer.

Where it is used

Typical applications.

  1. 01

    Plastering and rendering. Mesh as a key and crack-control layer over joints and chases.

  2. 02

    Poultry and livestock. Light enclosures where flexibility helps rather than hinders.

  3. 03

    Screening. Window, vent and opening screens against birds and debris.

  4. 04

    Garden and nursery. Plant protection and light boundary work.

  5. 05

    Sieving and separation. Coarse separation in yards and small works.

Buying guide

Woven or welded: pick by whether it must stand up.

Woven mesh interlocks the wires without fixing them, so the sheet stays flexible and can be rolled. Welded mesh fuses every intersection, so the sheet is rigid and flat and will not sag between supports.

If the mesh is being fixed to a wall, a frame or a surface, woven is usually cheaper and easier to work with. If the mesh is the structure, or must stay flat across a span, specify welded.

Both are quoted by area, so compare at the same aperture and the same gauge. A wider aperture at a lighter gauge covers more area per kilogram and costs less, for exactly that reason.

Questions before an enquiry

Useful answers.

What is the difference between wire mesh and weld mesh?

Wire mesh is woven, so the wires interlock and the sheet stays flexible, which suits screening, plastering and poultry work. Weld mesh is welded at every intersection, making a flat rigid panel that will not sag.

What apertures are available in woven wire mesh?

Commonly ½" to 2" in the woven range. Confirm the aperture, gauge and roll size with your enquiry.

Is wire mesh supplied galvanized?

Yes, the standard supply is galvanized. The coating grade is confirmed during quotation.

How is wire mesh priced?

By area or by weight depending on the product. Freight is quoted separately from the material.
